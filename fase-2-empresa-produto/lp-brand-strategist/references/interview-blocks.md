# Interview Blocks — Brand Deep-Dive

## How to Use This File

Present each block as a group of questions. Wait for answers before moving to the next block.
Adapt phrasing to the user's tone. Skip questions already answered in conversation context.

---

## Block 1 — Company Identity

Ask these together:

```
1. What's your company name and what do you do in ONE sentence?
   (Not the mission statement — how would you explain it to a stranger at a bar?)

2. How long have you been operating? How many people on your team?

3. What's your current revenue and number of active clients?
   (Approximate is fine — I need real numbers, not "many" or "several")

4. Where are you based? Do you serve clients locally, nationally, or internationally?

5. What industry/industries do you serve?
   If multiple: Which ONE industry represents most of your revenue?
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "You said [industry] — what specific type of companies? Size? Role of the buyer?"
- "When you say [number] clients, are these active paying clients or total historical?"

---

## Block 2 — Founder Story

Ask these together:

```
1. What's YOUR story? Where did you come from, what was your career before this?

2. What was the specific moment or event that made you start this company?
   (Not "I saw an opportunity" — what actually happened? What frustrated you?)

3. What's your biggest professional achievement BEFORE starting this company?

4. What's the hardest thing you've faced since starting? How did you handle it?

5. What's the achievement you're most proud of with this company?
   (Give me a specific number or story, not a feeling)
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "You mentioned [experience] — how many years? What was your title/role?"
- "That's a great story but I need more specifics — what were the exact numbers?"
- "What would your former boss/colleagues say about you?"

---

## Block 3 — Differentiation & Positioning

Ask these together:

```
1. Who are your top 3 competitors? (Names and URLs if possible)

2. If a prospect is comparing you to [competitor], what do you tell them?
   What's the ONE thing that makes them choose you?

3. What do your BEST clients say about working with you?
   (Exact quotes if you have them — even approximate ones from memory)

4. What type of client is a TERRIBLE fit for you? Who should NOT hire you?

5. If you had to describe your approach in 3 words, what would they be?
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "You said you're different because [reason] — can you give me a specific example with a real client?"
- "That sounds like what [competitor] also says — what SPECIFICALLY makes your version different?"
- "'Quality' and 'service' aren't differentiators — everyone says that. Dig deeper."

---

## Block 4 — Brand Voice & Visual Identity

Ask these together:

```
1. How would you describe the tone of your brand?
   Options to help: Professional / Casual / Technical / Provocative /
   Inspirational / Authoritative / Friendly / Rebellious

2. Give me 3 words you LOVE using to describe your work.
   And 3 words you would NEVER use.

3. Name a brand (any industry) whose communication style you admire. Why?

4. Do you have existing brand assets?
   - Logo (file format?)
   - Brand colors (hex codes or just descriptions?)
   - Specific fonts you use?
   - Brand guidelines or manual?

5. Do you have professional photos of yourself, your team, or your work?
   If not, are you willing to create them?
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "You said [tone] — show me an example. A post, a message, an email you sent that captures this."
- "Do you have the hex codes for your colors, or just general descriptions?"

---

## Block 5 — Social Proof & Trust Signals

Ask these together:

```
1. What's the most impressive result you've delivered for a client?
   (Specific: "We did X and the result was Y in Z time")

2. Do you have client testimonials? In what format?
   (Written quotes, video, screenshots, LinkedIn recommendations?)

3. Can you share client names/logos publicly? If not, can you describe
   them by industry and size? (e.g., "a logistics company with 60 employees")

4. Do you have any certifications, awards, partnerships, or media mentions?

5. What's your growth timeline?
   Key milestones with approximate dates:
   (Founded → First client → First milestone → Current state)
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "You mentioned [result] — do you have the exact before/after numbers?"
- "Can you get permission from any client to use their name? Even one makes a huge difference."
- "Any LinkedIn recommendations you could screenshot?"

---

## Extraction Checklist

After all blocks, ensure you have:

| Category | Minimum Data |
|----------|-------------|
| Identity | Name, industry, size, location, revenue, client count |
| Founder | Career history with years, turning point story, top achievements |
| Positioning | 3+ competitors, clear differentiator, ideal/anti-ideal client |
| Voice | Tone defined, word preferences, brand reference, aggression level (1-10) |
| Visuals | Logo status, colors, fonts, photos — what EXISTS vs what's MISSING |
| Proof | At least 3 quantifiable results, testimonial format, logo permissions |
| Timeline | 4+ milestones with dates for growth narrative |

If any category is incomplete, ask targeted follow-up questions before generating the brief.
