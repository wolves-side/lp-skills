### What to Study

1. **Color usage** — How do premium sites use accent colors sparingly?
2. **White space** — How much breathing room between elements?
3. **Animation pacing** — How fast/slow do elements appear?
4. **Background depth** — What subtle effects create richness?
5. **Typography scale** — How dramatic is the size difference between H1 and body?
