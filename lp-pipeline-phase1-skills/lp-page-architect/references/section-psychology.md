# Scroll Psychology & Conversion Flow

## The Reader's Psychological Journey

A landing page visitor doesn't read linearly — they scan, stop, engage, and decide.
Each section must meet them where they ARE psychologically and move them to the next state.

```
SCROLL DEPTH    MENTAL STATE              SECTION ROLE
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
0%              "What is this?"           → HERO: Hook + promise
                Skeptical, distracted     
                Will leave in 3 sec       

10%             "I have this problem"     → PROBLEM: Mirror their pain
                Starting to engage        
                Reading more carefully    

25%             "How does this work?"     → SOLUTION: Reveal mechanism
                Curious but skeptical     
                Looking for credibility   

40%             "Prove it"                → PROOF: Evidence stack
                Want to believe           
                Scanning for numbers      

55%             "What do I actually get?" → FEATURES: Deliverables
                Evaluating value          
                Comparing mentally        

65%             "Is it worth the price?"  → COMPARISON: Anchor value
                Calculating ROI           
                Thinking of alternatives  

75%             "Do others trust them?"   → TESTIMONIALS: Peer validation
                Almost convinced          
                Looking for reassurance   

85%             "What's the deal?"        → OFFER: Complete offer + CTA
                Decision mode             
                Ready if risk is low      

90%             "But what about..."       → FAQ: Handle last doubts
                One foot in, one out      
                Looking for red flags     

95%             "Last chance..."          → FINAL CTA: Close
                Decided or leaving        
                Needs final push          

100%            "Who is behind this?"     → FOOTER: Trust + contact
                Checking legitimacy       
                Looking for real humans   
```

## Key Principles

### 1. The 3-Second Rule
50% of visitors leave in 3 seconds. The hero must pass THREE tests simultaneously:
- **Relevance**: "Is this for me?" (subheadline answers this)
- **Value**: "What do I get?" (headline answers this)
- **Credibility**: "Can I trust this?" (trust anchor answers this)

### 2. The Proof-Before-Price Rule
NEVER show pricing before showing proof. The prospect needs to believe
in the value BEFORE they evaluate the cost. Exception: low-ticket items
where price IS the hook (e.g., "$9/month" SaaS).

### 3. The Visual Rhythm Rule
Alternate between light and dark sections to create reading rhythm:
```
Hero: DARK (high contrast, attention-grabbing)
Problem: LIGHT (comfortable reading)
Solution: LIGHT (continuation)
Proof: DARK (visual break, signals importance)
Features: LIGHT (scannable)
Comparison: LIGHT (table readability)
Testimonials: LIGHT or ACCENT (warmth)
Offer: DARK (high stakes, attention)
FAQ: LIGHT (comfortable, trust)
Final CTA: DARK or ACCENT (urgency)
Footer: DARK (standard, signals page end)
```

### 4. The CTA Timing Rule
Place CTAs at DECISION MOMENTS, not randomly:
- **After hero** (for the 5% who are already convinced)
- **After proof** (for the 20% who just need evidence)
- **After offer** (for the majority who evaluate the full picture)
- **After FAQ** (for the cautious who needed every question answered)

### 5. The Mobile Scroll Depth Rule
Mobile users scroll MORE but engage LESS per section.
Sections need to be SHORTER on mobile, not just narrower.
Long paragraphs → short paragraphs. Wide tables → stacked cards.

## Section Removal Guide

Not every LP needs all 11 sections. Remove based on LP type:

| LP Type | Remove | Why |
|---------|--------|-----|
| Lead Capture | Comparison, Offer Block, Testimonials (if none) | Short page, low commitment |
| Waitlist | Problem, Features, Comparison, FAQ | Minimal page, just hook + signup |
| Event Registration | Comparison, Trust Footer (simplify) | Focus on event value + FOMO |
| Service Sale | — (keep all) | High-ticket = high trust needed |
| Direct Sale | — (keep all, add bonus stack) | Full persuasion needed |
| Community | Problem (lighten), Comparison | Focus on belonging + FOMO |
| Portfolio | Problem, Offer, FAQ, Comparison | Not selling, just showing |

## Scroll Depth Benchmarks

For calibrating section length and priority:

| Scroll depth | % of visitors who reach it | Implication |
|-------------|--------------------------|-------------|
| 0% (hero) | 100% | MUST be perfect |
| 25% | 65-75% | Still strong attention |
| 50% | 40-55% | Core engaged audience |
| 75% | 25-35% | Seriously interested |
| 100% | 15-25% | Most committed prospects |

**Everything above 50% serves the most engaged visitors.**
Front-load your best content in the first 50%.
