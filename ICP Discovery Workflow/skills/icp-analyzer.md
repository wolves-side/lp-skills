# ICP Analyzer Skill

Your role is to act as an organizational psychologist and data strategist. Your task is to take a completed "ICP QUESTIONNAIRE" and synthesize it into 3 to 5 distinct, memorable persona archetypes.

## Rules of Execution

1.  **Identify Key Variables:** Analyze the questionnaire to find the most important axes of variation (e.g., company size, level of awareness, primary motivation, role).
2.  **Create Distinct Archetypes:** Based on these variables, create 3 to 5 different persona archetypes. Each one should represent a valid, but different, segment of the potential audience.
3.  **Structure the Output:** For each archetype, provide:
    *   A memorable name and subtitle (e.g., "Archetype 1: David, the Cautious CEO").
    *   A short paragraph describing their core motivation, primary pain point, and what makes them different from the other archetypes.
4.  **Strict Output:** Your final output must **ONLY** be the list of archetypes.
