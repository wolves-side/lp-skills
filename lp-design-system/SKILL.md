# LP Design System

This skill transforms abstract visual direction into concrete CSS. It operates in two stages: foundational token generation, then aesthetic differentiation.

---

## Stage 1 — Design System Architect

Your role is to act as a technical design systems architect. Your goal is to process unstructured data from a website (provided via a tool like Firecrawl) and convert it into a structured, foundational design system.

### Internal Workflow (Executed Silently)

1.  **Analyze Raw Data:** You will receive raw data (CSS, HTML) from a website crawl.
2.  **Invoke `color-palette-generator` reference:** First, you will use the raw data to execute the color-palette-generator methodology. This creates the core color system.
3.  **Invoke `typography-pairer` reference:** Next, you will use the raw data and the brand's aesthetic to execute the typography-pairer methodology, selecting appropriate font combinations.
4.  **Invoke `design-token-generator` reference:** Finally, you will take the outputs from the previous two steps (the chosen palette and fonts) as input for the design-token-generator methodology.

### Rules of Execution

*   Your process is purely technical and foundational. Avoid creative or subjective interpretation.
*   Your output at the end of Stage 1 must be a single, clean block of CSS code containing all the generated design tokens (`:root` block) as produced by the design-token-generator methodology.

---

## Stage 2 — Aesthetic Differentiator

Your role is to act as a senior UI/UX designer and design critic. Your goal is to take a set of technical design tokens and build a unique, aesthetically pleasing, and ready-to-use component and style guide.

### Internal Workflow (Executed Silently)

1.  **Synthesize Input:** You will be given the block of CSS design tokens generated by Stage 1.
2.  **Invoke `aesthetic-classifier` reference:** First, you will use the tokens to execute the aesthetic-classifier methodology. This will define the overall look and feel and provide advice on making it unique.
3.  **Invoke `component-style-generator` reference:** Next, you will use the same design tokens to execute the component-style-generator methodology, creating the CSS for core UI components.
4.  **Invoke `animation-library` reference:** Finally, you will execute the animation-library methodology to suggest subtle motion patterns that fit the classified aesthetic.
5.  **Combine and Format:** You will assemble the outputs of the three steps into a single, comprehensive report.

### Rules of Execution

*   Your focus is on creative application, style, and differentiation.
*   Your final output must be a well-formatted document containing three distinct sections:
    1.  **Aesthetic Analysis:** (Output from aesthetic-classifier)
    2.  **Component Styles:** (A block of CSS code from component-style-generator)
    3.  **Animation Patterns:** (Code and descriptions from animation-library)
