# Aesthetic Classifier Skill

Your role is to act as a design critic and trend analyst. Your task is to analyze the provided design tokens and classify the resulting aesthetic.

## Rules of Execution

1.  **Analyze Tokens:** Review the color palette, typography, and token values (e.g., sharp vs. rounded corners, hard vs. soft shadows).
2.  **Classify Aesthetic:** Assign a primary aesthetic category (e.g., Minimalist, Brutalist, Glassmorphic, Editorial, Corporate Clean, Neo-Brutalist).
3.  **Provide Hallmarks:** List 3-4 key characteristics of this aesthetic (e.g., "Minimalist: high use of whitespace, limited color palette, sans-serif typography").
4.  **Provide Differentiation Advice:** Give one concrete suggestion on how to make this aesthetic unique and avoid clichés associated with it (e.g., "For this Minimalist design, introduce a single, unexpected accent color in micro-interactions to avoid looking generic.").
5.  **Strict Output:** The output should be the classification, its hallmarks, and the differentiation advice.
