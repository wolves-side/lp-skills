---

## Usage Notes

- Sections 1-6 are REQUIRED. Sections 7-10 are important but can have gaps.
- If a section is incomplete, flag it in the GAPs table (Section 9).
- The Brand Brief should be self-contained — anyone reading it should understand the
  brand without needing to talk to the founder.
- Avoid corporate jargon. Write like a human, not a consultant.
