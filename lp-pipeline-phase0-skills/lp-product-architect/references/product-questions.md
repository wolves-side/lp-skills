# Product Deep-Dive Questions

## How to Use

Present each block as a group. Adapt phrasing to the LP type (see lp-types.md).
Skip questions already answered from the Brand Brief.

---

## Block 1 — The Product/Service

```
1. What exactly are you selling/offering on this page?
   Describe it as if explaining to someone who has never heard of your industry.

2. What are the 3-5 main things the client gets? (Features / deliverables)
   Not benefits yet — just what's literally included.

3. How long does it take for the client to see results?
   (First result, not full completion. E.g., "first report in 48 hours")

4. What's your price? (Or price range, or "starts at", or "custom quote")
   If custom: what's the typical range for your most common project?

5. How does pricing work?
   (One-time, monthly, per-project, retainer, freemium, tiered?)
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "You listed [feature] — what does that MEAN for the client in practice?"
- "You said [timeframe] — is that guaranteed or typical? What's the fastest you've done it?"

---

## Block 2 — The Transformation

This is the CORE of the landing page copy. Dig deep here.

```
1. BEFORE: What's life like for your client BEFORE they use your product/service?
   (Be specific: what are they doing manually? What frustrates them daily?
    What keeps them up at night about this problem?)

2. AFTER: What's life like AFTER? Paint the picture.
   (What changed? What do they no longer worry about? What can they now do?)

3. PROOF: Who has already gone through this transformation?
   Name a specific client or situation with real numbers.
   "Client X was [BEFORE situation] → after [timeframe] they were [AFTER situation]"

4. MECHANISM: Why does YOUR approach work?
   What's the specific method, technology, framework, or process that produces results?
   (This is your "unique mechanism" — the thing competitors can't easily copy)

5. Who is this NOT for? What kind of person/company would waste their money buying this?
   (Disqualifiers BUILD trust — they show you're not desperate for any sale)
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "You described the BEFORE as [generic pain] — give me a DAY IN THE LIFE. What does Monday morning look like for someone with this problem?"
- "The AFTER sounds great but I need a number. What specifically improved? Revenue? Time? Errors? Conversions?"
- "Why does this work and not [obvious alternative]? What's different about YOUR approach?"

---

## Block 3 — Objections & Customer Language

```
1. When someone is interested but DOESN'T buy, what do they say?
   List the top 3-5 reasons people hesitate or say no.

2. For each objection: what's your best response?
   (The thing you say that usually changes their mind)

3. How do your clients DESCRIBE the problem before finding you?
   (Their exact words — not your industry jargon.
    E.g., they say "my reports take forever" not "business intelligence latency")

4. What did they TRY before finding you? Why did those solutions fail?
   (Previous vendors, DIY attempts, workarounds, spreadsheets, etc.)

5. If your best client had to sell your product FOR you to a friend,
   what would they say in one sentence?
```

**Probe if needed:**
- "'It's too expensive' — compared to WHAT? What are they comparing your price to?"
- "You said they tried [alternative] — why specifically did it fail? What was missing?"
- "That sentence is too corporate. How would they ACTUALLY say it over coffee?"

## Extraction Summary

After all blocks, you should have:

| Element | Captured? |
|---------|-----------|
| Product name and clear description | ☐ |
| 3-5 features/deliverables | ☐ |
| Time to first result | ☐ |
| Price or price range | ☐ |
| Pricing model | ☐ |
| BEFORE state (specific, emotional) | ☐ |
| AFTER state (specific, measurable) | ☐ |
| Proof (at least 1 real example with numbers) | ☐ |
| Unique mechanism | ☐ |
| Disqualifiers (who should NOT buy) | ☐ |
| Top 5 objections with responses | ☐ |
| Customer language (their words, not jargon) | ☐ |
| Failed alternatives they've tried | ☐ |
| One-sentence client pitch | ☐ |
